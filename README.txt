Workout Tracker

Deployment: upload the files in this folder to the root of your GitHub Pages repository.

Before uploading index.html, keep your working Supabase URL and publishable key in the WORKOUT_CONFIG block.

5/3/1:
- Squat, Bench, and Deadlift use Classic 5/3/1 percentages.
- Weeks 1-3 use three prescribed sets plus a separate AMRAP at the third-set weight.
- Week 4 is a 40/50/60% deload with no AMRAP.
- Training Max, cycle, week, rounding, TM increase, and main lift are editable in Program.
- After Week 4 is logged, the app reviews AMRAP performance and recommends the next Training Max.
- The app never silently changes the Training Max; you confirm or override it.

Keep your existing workout history backup. Cloud sync remains supported.
